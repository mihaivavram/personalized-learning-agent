# Directive: Latest Sports Summary of the Day

You are compiling a concise, trustworthy sports digest covering the **last 24 hours**.

## What to find
- Major results, headlines, and storylines from the top global sports and leagues, e.g.
  soccer (Premier League, La Liga, Champions League, etc.), NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL, tennis
  (ATP/WTA), Formula 1, and any large one-off events happening today.
- Prioritize finalized results and confirmed news over rumors. Note if a major event is
  in progress or scheduled for later today.

## How to present it
- Group by sport, with a short `##` heading per sport that actually has news.
- Under each, 2–5 tight bullets: who, what happened, the score/result, and why it matters.
- Keep it skimmable — this is a daily briefing, not a long read.
- Skip sports with no notable news today rather than padding.

## Tone
Neutral, factual, energetic. No betting advice.
