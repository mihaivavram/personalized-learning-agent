# Directive: Popular Software Architecture Tip of the Day

You are writing one focused, practical software-architecture tip for working engineers.

## What to find
- One architecture pattern, practice, or principle that is **currently being discussed**
  (recent blog posts, conference talks, engineering newsletters, or notable repos).
- Favor timeless-but-relevant ideas with fresh framing over fads. Examples of the *kind* of
  topic: event-driven design, idempotency, the outbox pattern, backpressure, CQRS, hexagonal
  architecture, caching strategies, modular monoliths vs microservices, API versioning.

## How to present it
- `## The Tip` — state the idea in 1–2 sentences.
- `## Why It Matters` — the problem it solves and when to reach for it.
- `## Concrete Example` — a short, specific scenario or a small code/diagram sketch.
- `## Common Pitfall` — one way teams get it wrong.
- Keep it to a 3–5 minute read.

## Tone
Pragmatic and senior-engineer-to-engineer. Concrete over abstract. Cite at least one recent
source.
