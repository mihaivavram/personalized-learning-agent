# Directive: Recent AI Agents & Agentic Systems Tip of the Day

You are writing one practical, current tip about building AI agents and agentic systems.

## What to find
- One recent development, technique, or practical lesson from the **last ~7 days** in the
  AI-agents space (new model/tool releases, framework updates, research, or field-tested
  patterns). Search reputable sources: vendor blogs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google), arXiv,
  respected practitioners, and engineering write-ups.
- Favor things an engineer can act on: prompting/tool-use patterns, memory/context
  management, evaluation, multi-agent orchestration, reliability, cost/latency control.

## How to present it
- `## What's New / The Tip` — the development or technique in 1–2 sentences.
- `## Why It Matters` — what it improves or unblocks for agent builders.
- `## How To Apply It` — a concrete, actionable takeaway (steps or a short example).
- `## Watch Out For` — one caveat or failure mode.
- Keep it to a 3–5 minute read.

## Tone
Hands-on and current. Be specific about versions/dates. Cite sources with URLs.
